# Privacy policy — Borescope Direct

Effective date: 6 September 2026

Borescope Direct is designed to work locally on your Android device.

## Data handled

The app displays video from a USB camera after you grant Android's Camera and USB-device permissions. Photos and videos you choose to capture are stored in the app's local, app-specific folder. They leave the app only when you deliberately select Share and choose another app.

The app can create a compatibility report containing the device manufacturer/model, Android API level, USB vendor/product identifiers, attempted video profiles, and error text. The report excludes USB serial numbers, your media, location, account identity, and contact data. It is shared only when you deliberately use Android's share action.

## Data not collected

Borescope Direct contains no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, location access, microphone access, cloud upload, or developer-operated telemetry. It does not open your phone's built-in cameras.

## Purchases

Google Play processes the optional one-time purchase. Google may handle transaction and account information under Google's own privacy terms. The app stores a local unlock flag and queries Google Play for owned purchases; the developer does not receive payment-card details.

## Deletion

You can delete individual captures in the app. Android backup is disabled for the app. Uninstalling normally removes its app-specific local data, subject to device-vendor behavior.

## Children and medical use

The app is a general-purpose inspection tool, is not directed to children, and is not a medical device.
